01 / About

An engineer's approach to mobile.

Osama structures every app the way an architect drafts a building — presentation, domain, and data kept in their own layers, so features can grow without the codebase collapsing under itself. Three years in, that discipline shows up in apps used by thousands: attendance trackers with live GPS, multi-vendor marketplaces, and a FinTech invoicing platform built for regulatory compliance.

The other half of the job is what happens after the code compiles: pentesting storage and network paths with Frida and Burp Suite, pinning SSL certificates, and shipping hotfixes through Shorebird without waiting on a store review. Recently he's folded AI assistants and MCP tooling into planning and code review — treating them as another instrument on the bench, not a shortcut around the work.
